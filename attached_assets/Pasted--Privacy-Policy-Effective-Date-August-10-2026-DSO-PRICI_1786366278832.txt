# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 10, 2026**

DSO PRICING LLC (“DSO PRICING,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of visitors and users of dsopricing.com. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, protected, and otherwise handled when you visit or interact with our website.

When you use dsopricing.com, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, contact information, and information submitted through forms or other website features.

We seek to collect only information reasonably necessary to operate our website, communicate with users, provide requested services, and improve the user experience.

Information collected through dsopricing.com may be used to:

* Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website;
* Communicate with users about their inquiries or relationship with DSO PRICING LLC;

* Detect or prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or other harmful activity; and
* Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

DSO PRICING LLC takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or destruction.

## 5. Mobile Information and Text Messaging Privacy

No mobile information will be provided to outside organizations for marketing or promotional purposes.

Where applicable, users may reply **STOP** to discontinue text messages or **HELP** for assistance.
